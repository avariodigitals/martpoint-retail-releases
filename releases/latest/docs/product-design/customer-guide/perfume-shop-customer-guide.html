Adaeze’s House of Scents

Start here

Choose your path

MartPoint supports three levels of fragrance selling — from the simplest possible setup to a full blending lab. They build on each other: you can start at Path 1 and grow into Path 3 without throwing anything away.

Path 1 — Simplest
Sell what you buy

You purchase finished perfumes, diffusers or oils from a supplier and resell them. No formulas, no batches, no bottling. One product record per item, stock counts in bottles. Setup time: minutes.

Path 2 — One size
Blend your own, sell a single size (150ml)

You mix your own fragrance from base and oils. MartPoint tracks the blend as a batch, and you sell the finished liquid in one size — a 150ml bottle poured at the counter. Setup: raw materials + one sellable liquid + one formula.

Path 3 — Full lab
Blend your own, bottle many sizes (50 / 100 / 150ml)

Everything in Path 2, plus shelf-ready bottled SKUs. Bottling runs convert bulk liquid + empty bottles into sealed products, so one blend can fill three different shelf sizes. Adds: bottle items + bottled products + bottling runs.

The whole picture
Buy raw materials (Path 2/3) or finished goods (Path 1)
Formula — written once, says how much of each material one blend uses (Path 2/3 only)
Blend batch — consumes materials, produces bulk liquid in ml (Path 2/3)
Bottling run — converts bulk ml + empty bottles into sealed bottle SKUs (Path 3 only)
Sell — bottles at POS; stock decreases automatically
Enable the profile (Path 2 & 3 only): Settings → Business Profile → Perfumery & Blending — this unlocks fragrance units, maceration tracking and the wastage ledger. Path 1 shops can skip it entirely and use the standard retail profile.

All paths — buying stock

Purchase materials & finished goods

You never “buy a recipe” — a formula is written once for free. What you buy is the stock: oils, base, bottles, or finished products for resale.

Purchases → New Purchase → Fill in each field
Supplier
Pick or create the vendor (e.g. Lagos Fragrance Supplies)
Warehouse
Store — or your back-room/warehouse location
Items — add a row per product
Quantity is in the item’s base unit: raw materials in ml, bottles in pcs, finished goods in pcs. Unit cost per base unit.
Example — raw materials (Path 2/3)
Diffuser Base ×5,000ml @ ₦15 · Sweet Orange ×500ml @ ₦95 · Diffuser Bottle 150ml ×500pcs @ ₦450
Example — finished goods (Path 1)
Citrus Burst Diffuser 150ml ×24pcs @ ₦4,200 — the same screen, the same flow
Payment
Paid now, or record as credit and pay the supplier later

On save, stock increases by the purchased quantity and your cost basis updates. Raw material costs flow into blend costs automatically; finished-goods costs set the margin on resale.

“How do I purchase a recipe?”

You don’t. The formula is written once in Operations → Recipe Book (page 5) and reused forever. What you purchase is what the formula consumes — the oils, base and bottles. If a supplier sells you a formula on paper, you type it into the Recipe Book once; you still purchase the physical ingredients it calls for.

Next → every term, explained once — the glossary.
Reference

Terminology

Every word MartPoint uses for this workflow, in plain language.

Base UnitThe smallest unit an item counts stock in — ml for liquids, PCS for bottles and finished goods. Purchases, stock and alerts all use it.
Raw MaterialAn ingredient consumed by production: oils, base, alcohol. Always Not for Sale so it never appears at the till.
Not for SaleItem flag meaning “stock only, never sold.” Required on ingredients (formulas only list Not-for-Sale items) and on packaging. Never set it on your bulk liquid — the formula needs it sellable.
Bulk LiquidThe sellable product a blend produces, counted in ml — e.g. Citrus Burst (Bulk). Sold by the pour via Selling Units, or converted into bottled SKUs.
Selling UnitA named size on a sellable item with a conversion in base units — Bottle ×150 means “one sale removes 150ml.” This is where your sizes live on a bulk liquid. On a PCS item it’s for multi-packs only.
ConversionHow many base units one selling unit contains — 150 for a 150ml bottle, 10 for a decant, 6 for a gift box of 6 pieces.
DecantA small amount poured into a sample/travel container — sold as a selling unit on the bulk liquid.
FormulaThe written recipe in Operations → Recipe Book: ingredient quantities, expected yield, waste allowance, maceration days. Written once, reused every batch. Its Final Product names the bulk liquid it produces.
YieldTotal output one batch is expected to produce — e.g. 1,500ml. The batch credits this to the Final Product on completion.
Blend BatchOne production run of a formula. Starting it deducts ingredients; completing it adds the yield to bulk stock. Tracked by number (PB-0012).
MacerationCuring time after blending before the fragrance is ready. The Maceration Tracker lists vessels and their ready dates.
Waste AllowanceExpected % loss during mixing/pouring, declared on the formula so costing stays honest.
Wastage LedgerPerfumery → Wastage — where yield shortfalls and spillage are recorded with their cost, so losses are never invisible.
Bottled Product / SKUA shelf product in pieces produced by bottling runs — e.g. Citrus Burst — 150ml Diffuser. Never receives typed-in stock; only runs add it.
Empty Bottle ItemThe packaging item consumed per fill — PCS, Not for Sale, with Bottle Capacity (ml) set to its size.
Bottle Capacity (ml)Field on the bottle item declaring how much it holds. Bottling uses it to validate the fill.
Fill per BottleField on the bottled product: how many ml of bulk liquid one unit consumes.
Bottle / Packaging ItemField on the bottled product linking the empty bottle it uses.
Bottling RunRecorded on a completed blend batch: converts bulk ml + empty bottles into bottled product pieces. One stock adjustment, real unit cost, nothing double-counted.
Recipe / BOMItem-card field that links a product to a recipe for recipe-driven production (food-style). Fragrance products leave it at No Recipe (Manual Costing) — the formula’s Final Product link does the job instead.
Opening StockQty on the item’s Unit/Variant row — stock you already own on day one. After that, use Purchases to restock.
Alert QtyLow-stock threshold — the dashboard flags the item when stock drops to it.
