Profit & Loss, Cash Flow & Receivables

Profit & Loss Report

More → Reports → Profit & Loss Report. Shows income, cost of sales, expenses, and the final profit.

Sales incomeMoney from sales
Cost of salesWhat the sold items cost you
Gross profitIncome minus cost
ExpensesBusiness spending
Net profitWhat the business really made

Make sure purchase prices are accurate, otherwise profit numbers will be wrong.

Cash Flow Statement

More → Reports → Cash Flow Statement. Tracks actual cash in and out, not just profit.

Cash inSales, customer payments
Cash outPurchases, expenses, supplier payments
Net positionCash available

Receivables Aging

More → Reports → Receivables Aging. Shows who owes money and how long it has been unpaid.

CustomerWho owes

Stock, Item Sales & Reorder

Stock Report

More → Reports → Stock Report. Current stock levels and low-stock alerts.

BranchChoose location
CategoryFilter by product type
Stock quantityWhat is available
Alert quantityMinimum level set
Expiry alertExpired or expiring soon

Item Sales Report

More → Reports → Item Sales Report. Ranks products by quantity sold and revenue.

ProductItem name and code
Quantity soldUnits sold
RevenueTotal sales value
ProfitRevenue minus cost
TrendSlow or fast mover

Use this to decide what to promote, what to reorder, and what to stop stocking.

Reorder Suggestions

More → Reports → Reorder Suggestions. Lists products below their alert quantity and suggests how much to order based on sales history.

Current stockWhat you have
Alert levelMinimum allowed
Suggested orderQuantity to buy
SupplierDefault supplier if set

Use this before raising a new purchase order.

Other useful reports

  • Inventory Aging: stock that has not moved.
  • Sell-Through Report: how fast stock sells relative to what you bought.
  • Best Seller by Attribute: which variants (size, colour) sell best.
  • Sales & Payment Report: invoices matched to payments received.
  • Return Items: products customers returned.
