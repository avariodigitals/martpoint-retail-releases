Z-Report (Cashier Shift Close)

More → Z-Report (when cashier shifts are enabled)

End-of-shift reconciliation for each cashier. Shows expected vs counted cash, variance, and sign-off. The Z-Report is the single source of truth for what a cashier took in during their shift.

Shift codeUnique shift ID
CashierWho worked the shift
TillWhich till was used
Opened / ClosedShift start and end times
Expected cashOpening float + cash sales
Counted cashWhat the cashier counted
VarianceShort or over
StatusOpen or Closed

Pair with Open/Close Shift (More → Open/Close Shift) where a cashier opens with a float, then closes by counting the till and entering a manager PIN to sign off.

Getting the most from reports

A few habits make reports far more useful:

  • Set the date range first to match the question you are trying to answer.
  • Filter by branch or warehouse if you have more than one location.
  • Export to PDF, CSV, or Excel for accountants or managers.
  • Compare stock and sales reports together before placing large orders.
  • Reconcile cash in hand with the Cash Flow Statement every day.
