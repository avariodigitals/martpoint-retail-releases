Cashier workflow

What a cashier does every day

Your job is to ring up sales, collect payment, and make sure every customer gets a receipt. If a customer wants to pay later, return an item, or pause a sale, the system has buttons for those too.

Always do

  • Clock in before your first sale.
  • Scan or search every item.
  • Confirm the total before payment.
  • Print or email a receipt.
  • Keep cash safe and count it at shift end.

Never do

  • Give discounts without manager approval.
  • Sell on credit unless your role allows it.
  • Close the app during a sale.
  • Share your password.
  • Edit product details unless asked.

Before your first sale

  • Check the cash drawer — start with the correct float.
  • Test the receipt printer — make sure it has paper and is connected.
  • Clean the barcode scanner lens — dusty lenses cause failed scans.
  • Check that POS loads — open the screen and confirm it is not frozen.
  • Count your cash float — note it in the system if your store requires it.

Customer service at the counter

  • Greet every customer — a quick hello and eye contact.
  • Ask before scanning — “May I scan these for you?”
  • Say the total clearly — “That comes to N5,000”.
  • Offer the receipt — print or email it when payment is done.
  • Stay calm on queues — call for help if the line grows.

Cashier golden rule

Never hand over change before the customer has fully paid. Count the change out loud, and give the receipt before the customer leaves the counter. If a payment fails or is paused, do not let the customer walk away with the goods until the sale is saved.

POS — Quick checkout

POS is the fastest way to sell. Use it when the customer is at the counter ready to pay.

How to open

Tap the POS button on the bottom bar, or go to More → Sales → POS.

Screen sections

Search / barcodeType product name or scan
Running cartItems added and quantity
CustomerOnly if needed for credit
TotalAmount to be paid
Payment buttonsCash, Card, Transfer, Credit
HoldPause the sale

Step-by-step

  1. Tap the search box or click into it with the cursor.
  2. Type the product name or scan the barcode. Tap the product from the list.
  3. The product is added to the cart. Tap + or - to change quantity.
  4. Add more items until the cart matches what the customer is buying.
  5. Tap the payment method: Cash, Card, Transfer, or Credit.
  6. For cash, enter the amount received. The system shows change.
  7. Tap Save. The receipt prints or appears on screen.
  8. Give the receipt to the customer.

Payment buttons and methods

At the bottom of POS you see the checkout buttons. Choose the one that matches how the customer wants to pay.

PayPay the whole amount at once. Use for normal cash-and-carry sales.
Split PayPay with more than one method. Use when a customer uses any combination, such as Card + Transfer, Cash + Card, or even multiple non-cash methods. It does not have to include cash.
PayPlanPay in instalments. Use only when PayPlan is enabled and the manager has approved.

Then pick the method: Cash, Card, Transfer, or a custom method added by the admin. For cash, enter the amount received so the system can show change.

What happens if the screen locks

Your manager can turn on idle timeout. If the screen is still for a few minutes, a warning appears with a countdown. Tap Stay logged in before the timer runs out.

  • Snooze on: the screen pauses and shows a campaign. Tap it, then enter your password or PIN to continue.
  • Snooze off: the system logs you out when the countdown ends.
  • During a sale: tap Hold if you must step away, or finish the sale before the screen locks. A locked screen does not complete a sale.
Discounts: If a customer asks for a discount, do not enter it unless your manager has given permission. Some stores require a manager PIN.

Holds, Returns & Receipts

Hold — pause a sale

If the customer has to step away but wants to keep the cart, tap Hold and give the hold a name or number. Resume it later from Holds.

If your role allows returns, open the original invoice, tap Return, select the item and quantity, and choose refund method.

Quick fixes

  • Product not found: check spelling, scan the barcode, or ask the manager to add the product.
  • Wrong quantity: tap the item and change quantity before saving.
  • Wrong payment method: ask manager to edit the sale before end of day.
  • Receipt printer silent: check power, paper, and USB/Bluetooth connection.
  • Cash total looks wrong: do not close the sale. Re-check items and quantities.
  • Barcode scanner not working: type the product name manually or type the code on the product label.

End of shift

When your shift ends, count your cash and compare it with the system. If attendance is enabled, clock out. Do not close the browser or app while a sale is still open.
