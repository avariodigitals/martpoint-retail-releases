POS — Quick checkout

Use POS when the customer is at the counter ready to pay. It is designed for speed.

How to open

Tap POS from the bottom bar, or go to More → Sales → POS.

Screen sections

Item searchType name or scan barcode
Running cartItems, quantity, price
Customer (optional)Add a customer for credit sales
TotalsSubtotal, discount, amount due
Payment buttonsCash, Card, Transfer, Credit
Hold buttonPause the cart for later

Step-by-step

  1. Tap the search box and type the product name or scan the barcode.
  2. Tap the product to add it to the cart. Adjust quantity if needed.
  3. Repeat for every item.
  4. Tap a payment method: Cash, Card, Transfer, or Credit.
  5. For cash, enter the amount received; change is calculated.
  6. Tap Save. The receipt prints or downloads.
Next: Hold the cart if the customer steps away, or finish to Sales List.

New Sale — Invoice

Use New Sale when the customer may pay later, needs a formal invoice, or the sale requires more detail than POS.

How to open

More → Sales → New Sale or tap the New Sale quick action.

Screen fields

CustomerPick or add a customer
Sale typeCash, credit, or partial
Item linesProduct, quantity, unit price
Other chargesShipping, packaging
DiscountAmount or percentage
PaymentAmount received and method

Step-by-step

  1. Choose a customer. Leave as Walk-in if not needed.
  2. Pick the sale type: Cash, Credit, or Partial.
  3. Tap Add Item and search the catalog. Enter quantity and price.
  4. Add any other charges if the business allows it.
  5. Enter a discount if approved.
  6. Enter the payment received and the method.
  7. Tap Save. The invoice is created and the receipt is ready.
A manager PIN may be required for large discounts or credit sales, depending on permissions.

Quotation, Holds & Due Payments

Quotation — give a price before the sale

Use New Quotation when the customer wants a written price but is not ready to buy. Quotations do not reduce stock until converted.

CustomerRequired for the quote
Expiry dateHow long the quote is valid
Item linesProducts, quantities, prices
ConvertTurn quote into invoice

Open the saved quote and tap Convert to Sale when the customer accepts. Stock is then reserved.

Holds — pause a cart

Tap the Hold button on the POS or bottom nav. Enter a name or number, then save. Resume the hold later from Holds.

Hold nameHelps you find it later
ResumeTap to load cart again
DeleteRemove an abandoned hold

Due Payments — collect what is owed

More → Sales → Due Payments lists unpaid invoices. Tap one to record a payment.

Invoice numberReference the sale
CustomerWho owes the money
Amount dueOutstanding balance
Receive paymentEnter amount and method

Enter the amount collected, choose the payment method, and save. The balance updates instantly.

Sales List, Returns & Receipts

Sales List — view all invoices

More → Sales → Sales List shows every sale. Search by invoice number, customer, or date.

Filter by dateToday, week, month, custom
SearchInvoice number or customer
ViewOpen invoice details
Print / PDFReprint or email receipt

Tap an invoice to see items, totals, payments, and print a copy. You can also start a return from here if you have permission.

Sales Return — process a refund

From an invoice, tap Return. Select the items being returned, enter the quantity, and choose the refund method.

Return reasonWhy the item came back
Refund methodCash or credit to customer
Stock updateAutomatically adds items back

The return is saved and a return receipt is generated. Stock is updated automatically.

Receipt options

After every sale, you can print (if a printer is set up), download as PDF, or email to the customer. Choose from the action icons on the invoice.

Payments

Payment buttons, methods, and when to use them

At checkout you choose a button (Pay, Split Pay, PayPlan) and a method (Cash, Card, Transfer, or any custom method added by the admin). Pick the right one for each sale.

Payment buttons

These are the checkout actions. They decide how the total is paid, not how the customer handed over the money.

PayPay the whole invoice in one go. Use for normal cash-and-carry sales.
Split PaySplit the total across two or more payment methods. Use when a customer uses more than one method, such as Card + Transfer, Cash + Card, or any other combination. It does not have to include cash.
PayPlanPay in scheduled instalments. Use when the customer takes the items now but pays the balance over time. Only available if PayPlan is enabled for your store.

Payment methods

These describe how the money is collected. The admin can add custom methods in Settings.

CashCustomer pays with cash. Enter the amount received; change is calculated.
CardCustomer pays by POS terminal or bank card. Record the reference if required.
TransferCustomer sends money by bank transfer or mobile money. Ask for the transaction ID.
CustomAny method added by the admin, such as Cheque, Coupon, or Store Credit. Ask the manager which applies.
Always confirm the total before saving. If you choose Split Pay, the amounts across all methods must equal the invoice total. If you choose PayPlan, make sure the first payment and schedule are recorded before the customer leaves.
