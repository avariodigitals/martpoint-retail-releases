Automobile workflow

What a car dealership does every day

Your job is to keep vehicle records accurate, respond to buyer enquiries, and close sales without losing track of deposits and balances. MartPoint gives you vehicle-specific fields, photo galleries, and an online showroom built for cars.

Always do

  • Set the business type to Car Dealership first.
  • Fill in every vehicle field — buyers compare details.
  • Upload multiple photos for each vehicle.
  • Reserve a vehicle before accepting a deposit.
  • Mark a vehicle Sold only after full payment.

Never do

  • Sell a vehicle that another staff member has reserved.
  • Leave the status as Available after taking a deposit.
  • Upload blurry or dark photos.
  • Forget to enter the VIN and license plate.
  • Hand over keys before the sale is saved in the system.

Before your first vehicle

  • Set the business type — choose Car Dealership in Business Profile.
  • Check Vehicle Data — makes, models, and dropdown options.
  • Pick a storefront theme — Auto Modern, Auto Luxe, or Auto Garage.
  • Decide on Auto Parts — turn it on or off based on your business.

Customer service at the showroom

  • Respond quickly — reply to WhatsApp enquiries the same day.
  • Share the listing — send the vehicle page link on WhatsApp.
  • Be transparent — disclose condition, mileage, and history.
  • Reserve promptly — mark a car Reserved as soon as a deposit is paid.

Dealership golden rule

Never let two buyers pay for the same car. The moment a deposit is accepted, change the vehicle status to Reserved. This hides it from the online showroom and tells every salesperson the car is taken.

Morning

Set up the dealership

Chuka manages Platinum Motors. Before adding any cars, he tells MartPoint that the business is a car dealership and chooses the tools he needs.

Chuka, Dealer Principal

“We sell cars, not grocery items. I need vehicle-specific fields such as Make, Model, Year, Mileage, VIN, Condition, and Body Type. Some months we also sell a few parts, but I want parts to be optional.”

What Chuka does in MartPoint

  1. He opens More → Business Profile.
  2. He picks Car Dealership as the industry type. MartPoint automatically turns on the right features.
  3. He keeps Vehicles and Vehicle Services turned on.
  4. He decides whether to keep Auto Parts on or off. He leaves it off for now.
  5. He saves the profile. A new Automobile section appears in the sidebar.
Why the profile matters: Choosing Car Dealership gives you vehicle fields, a purchase order that talks about “Vehicle Purchases,” and online store themes built to sell cars. The labels and menus change automatically.

Prepare vehicle master data

Before adding cars, Chuka wants the dropdown lists ready — Toyota should only show Toyota models, and the body-type, fuel, and transmission lists should be clean.

Chuka, Dealer Principal

“I don’t want staff typing ‘Toyota Corrola’ in one record and ‘Toyota Corolla’ in another. If they pick Toyota, the Model list must only show Toyota cars.”

What Chuka does in MartPoint

  1. He goes to Automobile → Vehicle Data.
  2. He reviews the preloaded Makes such as Toyota, Honda, Ford, and BMW.
  3. Under Models, he checks that each model is linked to its make. Corolla is under Toyota, Civic is under Honda, and so on.
  4. He adds any missing makes and models, edits typos, and deletes ones he does not need.
  5. He checks Dropdown Options for Body Type, Fuel, Transmission, Condition, Drivetrain, and Colour, adding or removing values as needed.

What the master data controls

MakesToyota, Honda, Ford, BMW, etc.
ModelsLinked to a make — Corolla belongs to Toyota
Body TypeSedan, SUV, Truck, Hatchback
Fuel TypePetrol, Diesel, Hybrid, Electric
TransmissionAutomatic, Manual
ConditionNew, Foreign Used, Locally Used
Linked Make and Model: When a staff member selects Toyota in the vehicle form, the Model list automatically only shows Toyota models. This keeps records consistent and speeds up data entry.

Midday

Add a vehicle to stock

A 2005 Toyota Corolla has just arrived. Ada, the sales administrator, adds it to the system with all the details and a photo gallery.

Ada, Sales Administrator

“This car is Foreign Used, 45,000 km, grey, 1.8L engine, automatic, front-wheel drive. We bought it for N6.5m and we are selling for N8.5m. I need to upload the interior, exterior, and engine photos.”

What Ada does in MartPoint

  1. She opens Automobile → Add Vehicle.
  2. She selects Make: Toyota, then Model: Corolla, and fills in Year: 2005.
  3. She completes the specification fields: Colour, Mileage, Body Type, Fuel Type, Transmission, Condition, Drivetrain, Engine Capacity, VIN, and License Plate.
  4. She enters the Cost / Purchase Price and the Selling Price.
  5. Under Vehicle Gallery, she selects all the photos and uploads them. The first photo becomes the main image.
  6. She sets the status to Available and saves the vehicle.

What the form captures

Core detailsMake, Model, Year, Colour, Mileage
SpecificationsFuel, Transmission, Body Type, Drivetrain, Engine Capacity
IdentifiersVIN, License Plate, Vehicle Code
PricingCost price, Selling price, Status
GalleryMultiple photos — first one is the main image
StatusAvailable, Reserved, or Sold
Photo tips: Take photos in good daylight. Capture the front, rear, both sides, interior, dashboard, and engine bay. The first photo you upload becomes the main image on the online showroom and vehicle cards.

Afternoon

Handle a customer enquiry

A customer sees the Corolla on the online showroom and sends a WhatsApp message. Emeka, the sales executive, opens the vehicle record and replies quickly.

Customer (via WhatsApp)

“Hi, I saw the 2005 Toyota Corolla on your website. Is the price negotiable? Can I book an inspection?”

What Emeka does in MartPoint

  1. He opens Automobile → Vehicle List and finds the Corolla.
  2. He taps the WhatsApp link on the vehicle to send a reply from the dealership number.
  3. The customer likes the car and asks to reserve it. Emeka opens the vehicle and sets the status to Reserved, adding the customer’s name.
  4. He marks the car as reserved so no one else sells it while the customer finalises payment.

Vehicle statuses

AvailableShown on the online showroom — ready to sell
ReservedHidden from the public site — deposit taken
SoldHidden from the public site — sale completed
Why it mattersPrevents double sale and keeps the website accurate
Status keeps everyone in sync: Available, Reserved, and Sold statuses control what appears on the website and what the sales team can offer. A Reserved car is hidden from the public listing and protected from double sale.

Late afternoon

Sell the vehicle

The customer pays a deposit, then completes the balance. Emeka finalises the sale in the system and hands the car over.

Emeka, Sales Executive

“The customer paid N3m today and will bring the N5.5m balance next week. I need to record the deposit, show the balance, and print a receipt.”

What Emeka does in MartPoint

  1. He opens the Corolla record and clicks Sell Vehicle.
  2. He enters the Customer Name and the Amount Paid today.
  3. MartPoint calculates the Balance Remaining.
  4. He saves the sale. The vehicle status changes to Sold and the receipt is ready to print or send on WhatsApp.
  5. When the balance is paid, he records the final payment and marks the sale as fully paid.

What the sale updates automatically

Vehicle statusChanges from Reserved to Sold
Online listingRemoved from the public showroom
ReceiptGenerated with payment and balance
ReportsRecorded in sales and vehicle reports
Deposits and balances: Always record the deposit amount on the sale screen. MartPoint tracks the balance remaining so you and the customer can see exactly what is left to pay. Do not mark the sale as fully paid until the balance is settled.

Showcase vehicles online

Chuka checks how the dealership looks to customers online. The vehicles are listed, the gallery works, and WhatsApp enquiries go straight to the sales team.

Chuka, Dealer Principal

“I want the website to feel like a modern dealership. Fast loading, good on phones, and every car should have several photos that customers can click through.”

What customers see online

  1. They visit the dealership store URL and see the hero banner and trust badges.
  2. They tap Collection or Vehicles to see all available cars.
  3. They tap a car to open the detail page and click through the photo gallery.
  4. They tap Request on WhatsApp to ask about the car directly.
  5. Only vehicles with the status Available are shown. Reserved and Sold cars are hidden.

Choose a dealership theme

Auto ModernClean blue and white — fast and professional
Auto LuxeDark and premium — gold accents for luxury brands
Auto GarageBold red and charcoal — trucks, SUVs, performance
How to switchStorefront Theme settings in the admin
Speed and mobile: All three dealership themes use minified images and load fast on phones. The vehicle gallery switches thumbnails with one tap, so customers can browse every photo without waiting.

Quick fixes & support

Quick fixes

  • Vehicle not saving: check that Make and Model are selected — they are required fields.
  • Photos not uploading: keep each photo under 2MB and use JPG or PNG format.
  • Vehicle not showing online: confirm the status is Available and the online store is turned on.
  • WhatsApp button not working: check that the store WhatsApp number is set in Storefront Settings.
  • Model list empty: the model must be linked to a make in Vehicle Data first.
