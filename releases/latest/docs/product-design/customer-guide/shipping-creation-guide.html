Shipping Creation Guide

Create the delivery choices customers see online and the delivery fees staff select in-store. The same setup works for fashion, skincare, perfume, beauty, electronics, frozen foods and other retail shops.

Two places, two jobs. Online shipping is configured in Online Store settings. In-store shipping is configured in Sales → Shipping Fees. Set up each one separately.

Part 1 — Create shipping for the online store

1

Open Online Store

Go to Online Store → Settings. Open the Shipping & delivery area.

2

Add a method

Create a method such as Standard delivery, Express delivery or Store pickup.

3

Save and preview

Save the method, open the storefront in a private window, add an item and confirm it appears at checkout.

Method nameThe customer-facing name, for example “Lagos standard delivery”.
FeeUse a fixed amount when every order in the zone costs the same.
Quote at checkoutUse this when the courier price depends on the address or parcel.
EnabledOnly enabled methods appear to online customers.

For city-based pricing, turn on city shipping and add each city/zone with its fee. The checkout calculates the selected zone automatically.

Part 2 — Create shipping for the physical store

1

Turn on the feature

Go to Settings → Business Profile → Feature Flags and enable Manual Shipping (POS Delivery Fee).

2

Create delivery fees

Open Sales → Shipping Fees and click Add Shipping Fee for each service area.

3

Use it at checkout

At POS, add the items, choose Shipping / Delivery, select the area, then take payment.

NameWhat staff select, such as “Lekki delivery” or “Pickup”.
Location / areaOptional detail customers and staff can recognise.
FeeThe amount added as a separate shipping line on the receipt.
ActiveTurn a fee off without deleting it or changing old sales.

Shipping is added after products and before payment. The receipt and invoice show the location and delivery amount separately.

Daily use and checks

  • Online orders: open Online Store → Orders, confirm the shipping method and address, then fulfil the order.
  • In-store orders: select shipping before payment so the customer total includes the correct fee.
  • Pickup: create a zero-fee pickup method and keep delivery off for that order.
  • Price changes: edit the method or fee for new orders; saved invoices retain the amount already charged.
  • No shipping option: check that the feature is enabled and at least one method/fee is active.
Team rule: keep names consistent across online and in-store setup (for example, “Lagos standard delivery”). This makes reports, receipts and customer conversations easier to follow.
