Morning

Set up the boutique

Lola owns a small clothing store in Lagos. She sells dresses, tops, and accessories. Before the first customer walks in, she sets up the system so every size and colour is ready to sell.

Lola, Owner

“We just received a new shipment of the ‘Ankara Midi Dress’. It comes in Red, Blue, and Black, and sizes Small, Medium, and Large. I need each size-colour combination to have its own stock count.”

What Lola does in MartPoint

  1. She opens More → Inventory → Attributes and makes sure Size and Colour already exist.
  2. She goes to More → Inventory → Add Product and names the product Ankara Midi Dress.
  3. She turns on Has Variants, picks Size and Colour, and taps Generate Variants.
  4. MartPoint creates 9 rows: Small Red, Small Blue, Small Black, Medium Red, and so on.
  5. She gives each row a unique barcode from the clothing tags and enters the opening stock for each size-colour.
  6. She saves the product. The dress is now ready for sale at the POS.

Why variants matter for fashion

Without variants, Lola would have to create “Ankara Midi Dress Red M” as a separate product. With variants, the customer sees one clean product, but the system tracks every size and colour separately.

Midday

The cashier makes a sale

Ade is the cashier. A customer picks a Red Ankara Midi Dress in size Medium and pays with cash and a bank transfer.

Ade, Cashier

“Good afternoon. You chose the Red Ankara Midi Dress in Medium. That is N12,000. You said you are paying N7,000 cash and N5,000 transfer, right?”

Screen by screen at the POS

  1. Ade taps POS on the bottom menu.
  2. She searches Ankara and taps the product.
  3. MartPoint asks for the variant. She chooses Red and Medium.
  4. The system shows the price: N12,000.
  5. The customer asks for a 5% discount. Ade calls Lola, who enters her manager PIN to approve it.
  6. Ade taps Split Pay and enters Cash: N7,000 and Transfer: N5,000.
  7. She asks the customer for the transfer reference and types it into the transfer note.
  8. She taps Save. The receipt prints from the thermal printer.

What happens behind the counter

As soon as the sale is saved:

Afternoon

Returns, reports, and support

Later in the day, a customer returns a dress. Lola checks the sales report and then contacts support when she has a question.

Customer

“I bought this Blue Ankara Midi Dress in Large this morning, but it is too big. Can I change it for a Medium?”

How to handle the return

  1. Ade opens More → Sales → Sales List and finds the morning invoice by the customer’s name.
  2. She opens the invoice and taps Return.
  3. She selects the Blue Large dress, sets the quantity to 1, and chooses the refund method.
  4. She taps Save. The Blue Large stock goes back up by one.
  5. To give the customer the new size, she creates a new sale for the Blue Medium dress and applies the credit.

Checking the day’s numbers

At 5 p.m., Lola opens More → Reports → Daily Summary to see:

Total salesHow much the store made today
Best-selling colourRed dresses sold the most
Low stock alertMedium Black is running low
Cash positionCash in drawer matches the system

Need help? Reach support

Lola notices the receipt printer is not working. She opens More → Help → Support and sends a WhatsApp message to MartPoint support. She includes her store name and the issue.

While waiting, she emails the receipt to the customer and prints it later when the printer is fixed.
