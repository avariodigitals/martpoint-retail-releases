Barcode, Batch & Label Guide

Set up packaged sizes, use your assigned product barcodes, print accurate batch labels, and supply customers using MartPoint’s existing stock and sales screens.

Keep the two numbers straight: the barcode identifies the sellable product size. The batch or lot number identifies one production run. Different runs of the same size keep the same product barcode.
1. Product sizes2. Enter a barcode3. Download artwork4. Record batches5. Print labels6. Sell and supply

1. Set up each packaged size

  1. Open Items → Add Item and enter the main product name, such as Kilishi or Palm Oil.
  2. Choose Variants when package sizes need separate stock and prices.
  3. Add one variant for each size, such as Kilishi 100 g, 250 g and 500 g, or Palm Oil 500 ml, 1 L, 2 L and 5 L.
  4. Give each sellable size its own assigned barcode, SKU if used, cost, selling price and stock.

Use separate item records instead if that better matches your catalogue. The important point is that every size sold as its own product has its own stock identity and barcode.

2. Enter your assigned EAN-13

  1. Choose Retail barcode (EAN-13 / assigned GTIN) in the product barcode section.
  2. For one product, enter the number in its barcode field. For variants, enter a different number in each size row's Barcode field. Keep any leading zeroes.
  3. Save the product. MartPoint checks the length, digits, check digit and whether another product in your store already uses the number.
MartPoint does not issue official GS1 numbers. Get your GTINs from the appropriate source. Do not reuse one product size’s GTIN for another size.

Choose Internal barcode (Code 128) for existing internal codes and custom SKUs. POS scanning for existing Code 128 barcodes continues to work.

3. Download barcode artwork

  1. Save the product first.
  2. Open the product for editing. Its barcode section shows a preview and the available download links.
  3. Choose SVG for a scalable design file, PNG for an image, or PDF for a print-ready barcode file. Use Copy number when you need to paste the digits.
  4. Send the file to your packaging designer or printer.

Standalone barcode artwork contains the barcode and its readable number. It does not add MartPoint branding, store names or prices. If your packaging already has the correct barcode, keep using it; you can still print separate batch labels.

4. Record each production batch

  1. Open the exact item size and go to Batch / Unit Details. If batch or date fields are hidden, ask your store administrator to enable the matching batch, manufacturing-date and expiry features.
  2. Add a row for the production lot. Enter the lot number, manufacturing date, expiry date and quantity. Enter the correct costs and prices for that stock when needed.
  3. Repeat for the next production run. Keep the same product GTIN; enter the new lot number and dates on the new batch row.
Enter dates supplied by your production records. MartPoint does not invent manufacturing or expiry dates.

5. Preview and print labels

  1. Open Items → Print Labels and search for the exact product or size.
  2. Add it to the sheet and set the number of copies. If batches are available, choose the specific lot below the product name.
  3. Choose a label type and size, then click Preview Labels. Check the preview before printing.
Standard retail labelBarcode and product name, with optional price and store name.
Manufacturer packaging labelBarcode with the selected batch, manufacturing date and expiry date.
Barcode-only stickerFor already-printed packaging that needs a barcode sticker. Product name is optional.
Batch details stickerLot number and dates, with optional product name and no barcode.

Available sizes are 60 × 40 mm, 50 × 30 mm and 40 × 25 mm. Click Print / Save as PDF; choose your printer or Save as PDF in the browser print dialog. Set the printer media to the same dimensions.

6. Scan, sell and supply

Scan the barcode for the size being sold. MartPoint looks up that product or variant, and your existing POS, invoice, customer and stock workflows continue to apply. Record each size as its own sale line when supplying a supermarket.

Batch stock, expiry checks and stock rotation continue to use MartPoint’s existing inventory records. Barcode artwork and label printing do not change stock quantities.

Quick flow: Create product sizes → enter assigned GTINs → save → download artwork or print stickers → record production batches → select the correct batch for labels → scan and sell.

Browser print is supported. Printer compatibility depends on the printer, driver and label stock; confirm alignment with a test print before running a full batch.
