Service Items & Packages

A service is a non-stock product. You can sell it from POS, add it to packages, and include it in memberships.

How to open

More → Inventory → Add Product, then select the Service type. Or, on desktop, use Services to add a service item.

Screen fields

Service NameName shown on receipts
CategoryService category
PriceFixed or variable
DurationHow long it takes
Service bitMarks it as non-stock
Staff commissionPercentage per service

Step-by-step

  1. Open Add Product and choose the Service type (or open Services on desktop).
  2. Enter the service name, category, and price.
  3. Set the duration if your business books time slots.
  4. Enable staff commission if the person performing the service earns a percentage.
  5. Tap Save. The service is now available in POS and sales.

Memberships

Memberships let customers pay once and receive services over time. Perfect for gyms, salons, and spas.

How to open

More → Operations → Memberships.

Screen fields

Plan nameMembership plan name
PriceMembership fee
ValidityDays or months
BenefitsIncluded services or discount
CustomerWho owns the membership
Start / ExpiryMembership period
StatusActive or expired
RedeemUse a membership benefit at sale

Step-by-step

  1. Tap Memberships in Operations.
  2. First, create a Plan with name, price, validity, and benefits.
  3. Assign the plan to a customer. Set the start and expiry dates.
  4. Record the membership payment.
  5. At POS, the customer’s membership appears. Tap Redeem to use benefits.
