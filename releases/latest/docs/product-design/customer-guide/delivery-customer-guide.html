Overview

Pick the tool that matches the job

Delivery means different things in different shops. MartPoint separates them so each one stays simple.

Delivery fee at checkout — Manual Shipping

The customer buys items and pays a delivery charge on the same bill. The cashier picks a location — “Lekki — ₦2,000” — and the fee is added to the total and printed on the receipt as its own line.

Use it for: same-day dispatch, waybill/bike fees, any flat charge per area.

Delivery routes — Delivery Scheduling

Plan a run, assign a driver, and track each stop from scheduled to delivered. A printed delivery sheet gives the driver the full drop list.

Use it for: daily dispatch runs, wholesale drops, orders delivered after the sale.

Online orders — Storefront shipping

Your online store shows the shipping methods and notice you set in Online Store settings, and can offer pay-on-delivery to customers who order themselves.

Use it for: customers buying on your web storefront.

Setup — 5 minutes

Set up delivery fees once

A store owner or manager does this once. After that, cashiers just pick a location at checkout.

Step 1 — Turn the feature on

  1. Go to Settings → Business Profile → Feature Flags.
  2. Under POS, switch on Manual Shipping (POS Delivery Fee) and save.

Switch it off the same way to hide shipping from POS at any time — past receipts keep their shipping lines.

Step 2 — Add your locations and fees

  1. Open Sales → Shipping Fees.
  2. Tap Add Shipping Fee for each area you deliver to.
  3. Fill in the name, location and fee, then save.

Step 3 — Keep the list tidy

  • Tick Active off to hide a fee from checkout without deleting it — old receipts still show it.
  • Use Sort Order to put the most-used locations first.
  • Changed a price? Edit the fee — only new sales use the new amount; saved sales keep what the customer paid.

Charging delivery on a sale

After the items are in the cart, pick the delivery location. The fee lands in the total and on the receipt — no mental maths, no forgotten charges.

Step-by-step

  1. Add the customer’s items to the cart as usual.
  2. In the totals area, open Shipping / Delivery (desktop) or tap a shipping chip (mobile).
  3. Pick the location — the fee is added to the total immediately.
  4. Take payment as normal: cash, card, transfer, split, or PayPlan — the payable amount already includes shipping.
  5. Save. The receipt prints a Shipping line above the grand total.

What the customer sees

Subtotal₦18,500
Discount-₦500
Shipping (Lekki Delivery)₦2,000
Total₦20,000

Do

  • Pick shipping before entering payment — the payable amount updates itself.
  • Confirm the location out loud with the customer.
  • Use Hold & Resume freely — the shipping choice is saved with the held cart.

Don’t

  • Don’t type the delivery fee into Discount — that reduces the total instead of charging it.
  • Don’t rely on memory — if the fee is not on the receipt, it was not charged.
  • Don’t ask cashiers to guess — if a location is missing, a manager should add it under Shipping Fees.
Everywhere it works: the shipping picker appears on desktop POS, mobile POS, and the sale edit screen — and a held cart keeps the selected fee when recalled.

Records & the other delivery tools

Track shipping money and dispatch runs

Shipping on reports and invoices

  • Receipt / invoice: a dedicated Shipping line shows the location name and fee — on printed, POS, and WhatsApp invoices.
  • Sales Report: a Shipping column totals delivery revenue separately from product sales, so you can see exactly what delivery earns.
  • Editing a sale: reopen it and the saved location is pre-selected; change it or set it back to “No shipping”.

Delivery Scheduling — planned runs

  1. Enable Delivery Scheduling in Business Profile → Feature Flags.
  2. Open Operations → Delivery Scheduling and tap New Delivery Route.
  3. Add the stops (sales/orders), assign a driver, and set the status.
  4. Track stops through Scheduled → Out for Delivery → Delivered.
  5. Print the Delivery Sheet report for the driver’s drop list.

Online store orders

  • Set your shipping methods and notice in Online Store → Settings — customers see them at checkout.
  • Enable Pay on Delivery there too if you collect payment at the customer’s door.
  • Online orders land in Online Store → Orders for fulfilment like any other sale.

Quick answers

  • No shipping picker at checkout? The Manual Shipping flag is off, or no Active fees exist — check Business Profile and Sales → Shipping Fees.
  • Customer changed their mind? Edit the sale and pick a different location or “No shipping”.
  • Fee looks wrong? The fee is always taken from your Shipping Fees list at save time — fix it there once and every new sale is correct.
  • Delivery already paid separately? Leave shipping at “No shipping” and record it as a normal payment — nothing else to do.
