Customer walkthrough · 01

Your clinic, from arrival to follow-up

Open: Start hereResponsible: All staff
  1. Zonat PhysioSpace is our example physiotherapy and rehabilitation centre. It receives walk-in and booked patients, delivers outpatient sessions, supports residential rehabilitation, and procures therapy materials and equipment.
  2. Choose the journey: outpatient care follows reception → intake → assessment → plan → finance → session → next appointment. Residential care adds admission → room/bed → ward care → review → discharge.
  3. Use the patient record as the common thread. Each handover carries the patient identity, the next action, its owner and the outstanding task.
  4. All people, prices and transactions in this guide are training examples. Agree the clinic’s actual fees, staff authority and care policies before using them.

Agree packages, ward rates and opening records

Open: Clinical configuration → Session packages / Billing & ward policyResponsible: Manager with finance and clinical lead
  1. Create approved session packages from the care services. Our example is six rehabilitation sessions at ₦10,000 each: ₦60,000. The physiotherapist chooses the patient’s actual plan.
  2. Open Ward → Bed board. Under Add ward / bed, enter Rehabilitation Ward and click Add ward. In the bed form select that ward, enter R01, optionally enter a Rate override, and click Add bed. Repeat for R02. Saving each bed automatically creates its linked bed account; no separate account form is needed.
  3. Open Administration → Clinical configuration → Billing & ward policy (also available as Ward → Inpatient billing). Under Daily rates select the code, enter Rate name, Amount and Effective from, then click Save new version. Our example bed rate is ₦20,000 per chargeable day; R02 has a ₦25,000 bed-rate override. Add nursing rates only if the clinic charges them.
  4. In Charging policies review the fields: discharge_day_charge = none or full; leave_billing = full, half or none; morning_due / evening_due = task times; auto_settle_wallet = 1 to settle invoices from available funds, or 0 to leave settlement to finance. Confirm admission-day handling with the clinic’s billing procedure. Changed policy fields save when you leave the field; save rate versions with the correct effective date.
  5. If moving existing patients into the workspace, enter outstanding debt, unused funds and remaining sessions separately in Opening positions, with evidence and an as-of date. A different authorised reviewer approves them.
In Bed payment accounts, tick Enable bed accounts for this workspace to record new transactions by bed. It is off by default; saving a bed still creates its account. Staff need beds_manage to add wards/beds and daily_billing_run to edit billing settings. Patient billing and ward care continue with recording off.

Reception to intake

Open: Clinic → Appointments / Care queueResponsible: Reception → nurse
  1. For a booked visit, open the appointment and use its check-in action. For a walk-in, open Care queue → Walk-in Check-in, choose the patient and branch, enter a brief arrival note and select Check In.

Plan to bill and verified payment

Finance to session and next appointment

Open: Treatment plan → Schedule session / Clinic → PhysiotherapyResponsible: Reception → physiotherapist
  1. Open the plan’s session entitlements. Confirm there are remaining eligible units, then use Schedule session with the correct date, time and clinician.
  2. On arrival, confirm the patient and session. Select Check in, then the physiotherapist selects Start when care actually begins.
  3. Record the treatment delivered, response and relevant observations using the clinical notes available to the clinician. Select Complete only when the session has actually been completed.
  4. Check that the session is completed and the remaining session count has reduced. For Ada’s fully paid six-session plan, one completed session leaves five.
  5. Reception confirms the next appointment and uses Close Visit at the appropriate queue stage. Give the patient the agreed follow-up instructions and direct her to the exit.
For a fully paid package, completing a session uses a session entitlement; it does not collect the same fee again. Record a no-show or cancellation with the matching action, rather than marking care complete.

A day in the ward

Moving beds, leave and referral

Residential billing without bed accounts

Open: Ward → Inpatient billing; Patient accountsResponsible: Finance
  1. Leave Enable bed accounts for this workspace switched off. Confirm daily rates and charging policies are configured.
  2. Run today’s billing for the correct date, or check the clinic’s scheduled billing run. Review Daily charges on the admission and its linked invoice.
  3. Receive payment against the correct patient invoice. If automatic wallet settlement is enabled, review the wallet settlement entries and remaining invoice due.
  4. Explain the bill by patient and admission: charge dates, bed/nursing lines, amounts paid and outstanding balance. The Bed board still shows occupancy.
  5. At discharge, review final charges and the admission invoice. Record payment or an approved collection arrangement, and keep any unpaid balance visible for finance follow-up.
Switching bed-account recording off stops new assignments; it preserves history already recorded. Patient invoices, funds, receipts and admission charges remain the working financial records.

Residential billing with bed accounts

Open: Billing & ward policy → Bed payment accounts; Ward → Bed accountsResponsible: Manager enables; finance reviews
  1. Open Administration → Clinical configuration → Billing & ward policy → Bed payment accounts. Tick Enable bed accounts for this workspace; the page reloads after saving. Then open Ward → Bed accounts and select Rehabilitation Ward / R01. Accounts are created automatically when you click Add bed on Bed board. Outpatient transactions use the Outpatient account.
  2. Post and review charges through the normal patient/admission billing workflow. Each recorded charge keeps the bed account assigned when posted.
  3. Receive payments or record verified wallet funding through the normal patient account screens. Check the payment/deposit account recorded at that time.
  4. After a completed transfer, new transactions use the new bed account. Earlier charges and payments keep their original account history.
  5. For linked system accounts, open Billing & ward policy and tick Use system accounts for Physio billing. This separate option is off by default. Existing and new beds receive accounts under Accounts; switching off preserves history and stops new routing. On an inpatient plan/bill, Billing account shows the current bed account. On an outpatient plan, choose an active non-bed account before Create Bill; the payment screen uses that choice. Direct payments credit the system account book; wallet settlement does not create another collection. From the bed ledger, authorised staff can Open linked system account book. The patient invoice remains the balance owed.

Customer walkthrough · 16

Bed transfer: follow the numbers

Open: Bed accounts → R01 / R02 → linked patient invoiceResponsible: Finance
  1. Training example: Bola’s posted R01 charge is ₦20,000. He transfers to R02; a later R02 charge is ₦25,000. Assume both are valid chargeable days under the agreed policy.
  2. After the transfer, finance receives ₦45,000 against the admission invoice. That new payment credits R02 even though it settles charges from both beds.
  3. Open R01: service charges ₦20,000 and invoice payments ₦0 give an account activity balance of ₦20,000.
  4. Open R02: service charges ₦25,000 and invoice payments ₦45,000 give an account activity balance of −₦20,000.
  5. Open the patient invoice: ₦45,000 billed less ₦45,000 paid gives ₦0 outstanding. Explain this patient balance when discussing whether the bill is settled.
A ₦50,000 verified wallet deposit followed by a ₦45,000 wallet settlement leaves ₦5,000 available, assuming no other holds or movements. The deposit is collected money; the settlement applies that money to the bill. Do not count both as two cash receipts. Earlier transactions without a recorded account are excluded from bed accounts.
Before handing over

Customer walkthrough · 17

Room to discharge and gate departure

Open: Admission → Recommend discharge → DischargeResponsible: Physiotherapist → approver → ward / reception → finance
  1. The treating clinician records Recommend discharge with the clinical reason and hands it to the authorised discharge approver.
  2. The approver reviews the case and records approval or rejection. Approval requires the discharge reason and summary; record follow-up instructions and the follow-up date where applicable.
  3. Finance reviews the admission account and likely final balance. Explain settlement or the agreed outstanding-payment arrangement to the patient or responsible contact.
  4. When the patient actually leaves, the authorised colleague selects Record departure. Approval alone does not close the stay. Confirm the admission is discharged and occupancy has closed.
  5. Check the final billing result and receipt/statement. Give the patient the discharge summary and confirmed follow-up details through the clinic’s approved document process. Reception and the porter coordinate exit and gate handover.
Clinical discharge and financial collection are separate responsibilities. Unpaid invoice balances survive discharge. The final billing run follows recorded departure and applies the configured departure-day policy.

Actual departure is recorded; follow-up is clear and any debt remains visible.

Control treatment materials and equipment

Open: Therapy aids & material → item record; Purchases / ExpensesResponsible: Stock officer with therapy-room lead
  1. Count gel, tape, electrodes and other supplies in the same units used in the catalogue. Record the treatment room’s agreed replenishment level and review stock alerts.
  2. Keep a dated issue/count record of supplies moved into use. Use the authorised stock adjustment process for verified usage, damage or count corrections, with a reason and reviewer.
  3. If a patient is billed for a supplied aid, include the actual billable item through the supported billing workflow and check the stock outcome. Keep internal treatment consumption distinct from patient charges.
  4. For machinery, retain the purchase invoice, model, serial number, warranty details, location, responsible person and commissioning/service documents in the clinic’s equipment records.
  5. Plan servicing, log faults and remove unavailable equipment from the working room. Record servicing costs under Expenses and keep the service evidence for manager review.
Recording a completed therapy session is not evidence that gel or tape stock was issued. Check and record physical usage through the clinic’s stock procedure. Buying a machine records procurement; the clinic’s equipment register holds its operating and maintenance history.

Follow-up, documents and your first practice run

Open: Patients → profile; Appointments; Documents & consentResponsible: Reception with clinician and manager
  1. Book the next visit from the agreed plan or discharge follow-up. Confirm the date, time, location and contact details with the patient.
  2. Keep consent, assessment attachments and discharge documentation under the correct patient record and document category. Where the patient portal is enabled, invite the intended patient through the authorised portal process.
  3. Practice Ada’s complete outpatient path: identify → check in → intake → assessment/plan → bill/payment → schedule → start/complete → next appointment → close visit.
  4. Practice Bola’s residential path: admit R01 → ward tasks → transfer R02 → daily bill → discharge recommendation → approval → actual departure → final invoice review.
  5. Repeat the financial explanation with bed-account recording off and on. Then practice a gel/tape/equipment purchase and a part payment to its supplier.
  6. Ask the trainee to explain who owns each handover and locate the supporting screen. The manager signs off only when the trainee can reconcile the patient invoice and the example transfer accounts.
Quick language: appointment = planned visit; care queue = today’s handover stage; plan = agreed care; entitlement = sessions available to deliver; admission = residential stay; bed account = transaction activity assigned to a bed; patient invoice = bill and outstanding balance.
