02 · One-time setup
Store profile · units · locations

Set the plant up before the first purchase

Store ProfileZonat Processing LTD
1

Business name. Save Zonat Processing LTD as the store name. Use the plant address, phone and business email that buyers should see on documents.

2

Document identity. Upload the company banner for quotations and invoices, set the email reply-to address, then upload the authorised signature and turn on Show Signature if Zonat uses it.

3

Units. Make sure kilogram, litre, metre, piece, set and drum are available. Use one stock unit consistently for each item.

4

Plant location. Use a warehouse or depot for the main plant; add another only when stock is physically held elsewhere.

A supplier's proforma is an incoming purchasing document. Zonat's quotation is an offer to its buyer. They belong to different sides of the workflow.
Open Business Profile · Document Banner · Email Settings · Plant / Depot.

05 · Purchases
Supplier proforma → receipt

Procure materials, machinery and spares

Purchase · Supplier ProformaZonat Processing LTD
1

Choose supplier. Select the grower cooperative for fruit or Industrial Plant Spares for an expeller part.

2

Capture the preliminary document. Use Supplier Proforma for the supplier's offered quantities, part codes, units and prices. Save it as the preliminary purchase record.

3

Confirm the purchase. Compare the supplier's final terms with the proforma, including taxes and delivery cost where applicable.

4

Receive what arrived. Record the physical quantity against the correct item and plant location. If only part of the order arrived, keep the outstanding quantity visible.

5

Record payment. Match supplier payment to the purchase, then review the remaining supplier balance.

Example: the supplier offers 5 SET of EK-200K feed cages. Zonat receives 3 SET today. The team records three received sets and follows up on the remaining two.
Open Supplier Proforma · Purchases · Phone: Purchases.

07 · Process formulas
Plan a repeatable batch

Define expected inputs and output

Process FormulasZonat Processing LTD
1

Create a formula such as Crude Palm Oil — First Pressing and select the finished oil item.

2

Add expected input items: for example fresh fruit bunches and the process consumables actually used by this plant.

3

Enter expected quantities and the intended output unit. Keep the formula based on Zonat's approved process data, not a supplier's sales sheet.

4

Review the calculated expected cost, then save the formula for future runs.

The item catalogue also contains machinery and spare parts for procurement. A process formula lists the materials actually consumed to produce output.

08 · Processing runs
Production schedule

Run, review and complete the batch

Processing RunsZonat Processing LTD
1

Schedule a run for the chosen oil item and planned date. Give it a batch name the shift team can recognise.

2

Check that required input stock is available. Assign the planned quantity and begin the run.

3

At shift handover, compare actual output with planned output and record the operational result in the run.

4

Have the responsible team review product quality and quantities before completing the run. Completion posts the finished stock and consumes the recorded inputs.

5

Return to Items or Plant Stock to confirm the finished oil quantity is available for a buyer order.

Complete a run only after checking quantities. A completed batch should not be completed again just to increase stock; create the next run for the next production cycle.
Open Processing Runs · Phone: Production.

09 · Buyer quotation
Quote screen

Offer bulk oil or plant spares clearly

New QuotationZonat Processing LTD
1

Select the buyer, quotation date, expiry date and buyer reference.

2

Add a group heading such as Bulk Crude Palm Oil or EK-200K First Pressing Spares. Add the relevant item lines inside that group.

3

Use the item picker to bring in saved codes and descriptions, then confirm quantity, unit and unit price. For a one-off quoted part, enter its code and description manually.

4

Apply the correct tax selection, write payment and delivery terms in Notes, and save. Percentages such as 30% deposit remain intact.

5

Review the group subtotals and numbering, then print or open the PDF. Send it to the buyer by email only after confirming the buyer address and Zonat reply-to address.

The document banner is optional for this business profile. When enabled, it uses Zonat's branding at the top of the quotation and invoice. The authorised signature appears when an image is uploaded and Show Signature is on.
Open New Quotation · Quotations · Phone: Quotations.

10 · Invoice & payment
Buyer order to cash

Invoice what Zonat delivers

Customer InvoiceZonat Processing LTD
1

Open the accepted quotation and use Convert to Invoice, or create a customer invoice for a direct buyer order.

2

Check the buyer, delivered item, quantity, unit, unit price and tax. Match the invoiced volume to available finished stock and the actual dispatch.

3

Save the invoice, record any payment or deposit against it, and confirm the outstanding customer balance.

4

Share the invoice PDF with Zonat's name, contact details, banner or signature settings as configured.

Example: a buyer accepts a quotation for 2,000 litres of bulk crude palm oil. Zonat checks the available litres, dispatches the agreed quantity, invoices that quantity and records the payment received.
Open Customer Invoices · New Invoice.

11 · Daily walkthrough
A normal working day

From opening check to close of day

07:30
Dashboard. The plant manager checks open runs, inputs below reorder level, purchases awaiting receipt, active quotations and customer balances.
08:00
Suppliers & Purchases. Procurement checks today's fruit delivery and the expeller spare-part order. It records quantities received and leaves unreceived quantities outstanding.
09:00
Plant Stock. Stores confirms kilograms of fruit and kernels, metres of filter cloth and packaging pieces on hand.
09:30
Process Formula & Run. The supervisor schedules the first pressing run, checks input availability and starts the batch.
13:00
Run Review. The shift team records actual quantities and reviews the oil output before completing the batch.
14:00
Quotation. Sales prepares a grouped offer for the buyer with litres, price, tax and payment terms; the manager reviews the PDF before email.
16:00
Invoice & Payment. Accepted quantity is invoiced, the dispatch is checked against stock, and any buyer payment is recorded.
17:30
Close of day. The manager revisits the KPIs, remaining supplier receipts, open quotes, completed runs and outstanding customer balances.
On a phone, use the Processing Plant dashboard for the same daily checks, then open the relevant purchase, production or quotation screen. The store name stays visible in the mobile header.
