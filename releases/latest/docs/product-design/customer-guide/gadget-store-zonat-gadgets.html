Next → warranty — the claim that tests whether the serial work paid off.
After-sales

Serial, IMEI & Warranty — setup to after-sales

Use this workflow for a new product, a model already in the catalogue, and the customer who returns with a device for service.

Choose the right starting point
New product — add the model once under Items → Items List. In Unit / Variant Details, enable Require IMEI for phones or Require Serial Number for other equipment; set the warranty months to Zonat’s actual terms.
Product already in the catalogue — edit that same item, enable the tracking option and set the warranty months. Do not create a duplicate product for another delivery.
Add new stock — go to Purchases → New Purchase, select the existing item and choose Received when goods arrive. Expand Additional Details on each tracked line and enter one identity row per received unit.

Existing shelf stock without unit records: product settings do not attach identities to an old total quantity. Don’t create a duplicate purchase or adjust stock to force it; ask an administrator for an audited backfill.

The claim, step by step
Look it up — in Operations → Warranty Lookup, search by serial, IMEI 1, IMEI 2, invoice, customer or product. Confirm the final sale and saved coverage end date.
Open a case — an eligible final sale with active recorded coverage offers Open case. Record the reported fault.
Inspect and decide — move the case through reported, inspecting, approved/rejected or awaiting parts. An inspection decision note is required to approve or reject.
Resolve — record repair, replacement, refund or another resolution with a resolution note. Status changes are logged with staff, time and notes.
Coverage and stock are separate: a case records service handling; it does not automatically issue a refund, move stock or bind a replacement unit to the original sale. Use the normal return, refund and replacement-sale workflows for inventory and accounting. Keep the saved warranty duration aligned with the supplier/manufacturer terms Zonat honours.

Six smartphones, four UPS units and accessory stock arrive. Zonat sells through the counter, a reseller invoice and an online order while recording an installation handoff and after-sales check.

Reference

Terminology & setup checklist

Every word this workflow uses, in plain language.

Serial numberThe manufacturer’s unique ID on a device — laptops, UPS units, consoles. One per physical unit; captured on the purchase row and picked at the sale.
IMEI 1 / IMEI 2Cellular-device identifiers. A dual-SIM phone can have two IMEIs but remains one physical stock unit; record both on the same identity row.
Serialized itemA product flagged for serial number or IMEI tracking. Stock counts units, but each unit carries identity — required for phones with IMEI tracking, and computers/power equipment with Serial Number Tracking enabled.
Bulk itemAn untracked product — cables, cases, chargers. Stock is a number; sales take a quantity.
Selling UnitA declared pack on a bulk product — Pack of 10, Carton of 50 — with its own price. Stock always counts in the base unit (pieces).
Warranty PeriodThe product default duration. A final sale snapshots the duration and its start/end dates; cases can be opened only for an eligible final sale with active recorded coverage.
Warranty CaseA store-scoped service record with reported issue, current status, decision and resolution notes. Refunds, stock changes and replacement sales still use their normal workflows.
Wholesale priceThe reseller rate on each product. The POS wholesale button and account invoices apply it — no hand-typed discounts.
Manager approvalThe guard on discounts above a rep’s limit — the sale waits in Pending Approvals for an owner decision.
Branch / WarehouseWhere a unit physically sits — Showroom Floor sells, Stockroom holds. Transfers move stock between them with a full trail.
Price CatalogueThe clean sell-price list the profile enables — the shareable, printable answer to “how much is it?”
Public CatalogueA read-only storefront — catalogue, prices, stock states, WhatsApp CTA, no checkout. For shops that showcase but don’t sell online.
Store CreditRefund value held on the customer’s record instead of cash-out — applies to their next purchase automatically.
Gift CardA prepaid card sold at the till, redeemable in store or online — balance tracked on the card.
QR orderingA printed scan-to-shop code — walk-ins open your online store on their own phone.
