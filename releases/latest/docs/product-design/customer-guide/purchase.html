New Purchase

This is where you record a delivery or a supplier bill. The items you enter are added to your catalog once the purchase is saved.

How to open

More → Purchase → New Purchase or tap the Purchase Stock quick action on the dashboard.

Screen fields

SupplierPick an existing supplier or add new
Reference NoSupplier invoice or delivery note number
Purchase dateWhen stock was received
StatusPaid or Unpaid
Item searchFind products in catalog
QuantityHow many units arrived
Purchase priceCost per unit
Batch / ExpiryOptional tracking numbers and dates
Other chargesShipping, handling
DiscountSupplier discount
TotalsSubtotal, total amount
SaveSave and update stock

Step-by-step

  1. Select the Supplier. If not in the list, go to Suppliers and add them first.
  2. Enter the supplier’s reference number (invoice or delivery note) and the purchase date.
  3. Choose Paid if you already paid, or Unpaid to track the bill.
  4. Tap Add Item and search the catalog. Select the product.
  5. Enter Quantity, Purchase Price, and batch/expiry if your business tracks them.
  6. Add any other charges or discounts.
  7. Review the total and tap Save. Stock is added to the selected branch.
Next: view the saved purchase in Purchase History, then pay the supplier when due.

Purchase History

Every saved purchase appears here. Use it to find old supplier bills, add payments, or check what was received.

How to open

More → Purchase → Purchase History.

Screen fields

SearchReference number or supplier
Filter by dateToday, custom range
Status filterPaid, Unpaid, All
Purchase numberAuto-generated ID
Supplier nameWho you bought from
Amount / DueTotal and balance owed
ViewOpen full invoice
PayRecord a supplier payment

Step-by-step

  1. Scroll or search for the supplier or reference number.
  2. Tap a purchase to see the full invoice, items, and costs.
  3. Tap Pay to record a payment against that bill.
  4. Tap PDF to download or share the purchase copy.

Suppliers & Supplier Payment

Suppliers — manage who you buy from

More → Suppliers → Suppliers shows the list. More → Suppliers → Add Supplier creates a new one.

Supplier nameBusiness or contact name
Phone / EmailContact details
AddressDelivery or billing address
Opening balanceAmount already owed
Total purchasesLifetime buying value
Due amountCurrent outstanding balance

Add a supplier before making your first purchase. Once created, they appear in the New Purchase screen.

Supplier Payment — settle bills

When you pay a supplier, open More → Suppliers → Supplier Payment or tap Pay inside a purchase.

SupplierWho you are paying
Payment amountHow much you paid
Payment methodCash, transfer, cheque
AllocateApply to one or more bills

Enter the amount and method, then allocate it to open purchase invoices. The supplier balance updates immediately.

Purchase Return — return stock to a supplier

If stock is damaged or wrong, open the original purchase, tap Return, select the items and quantity, and save. Stock is reduced and a supplier credit or cash refund is recorded.
