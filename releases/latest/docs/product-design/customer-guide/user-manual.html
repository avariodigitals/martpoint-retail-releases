Quick start

Your first 5 minutes with MartPoint

Five simple steps from login to your first sale. Each step is one screen, one decision.

1

Open your store link and sign in

Enter your username and password. If your business has several branches, pick the one you are working in today.

2

Check the Dashboard

See today’s cash position, quick-action buttons, and any alerts (low stock, expiring items, or pending payments).

3

Open the POS

Tap POS on mobile or Sales → POS on desktop. The cart opens ready for items.

4

Add items to the cart

Scan a barcode or type the product name. Adjust quantity, apply a discount if allowed, then choose the payment method.

5

Checkout and print

Enter the amount received, confirm change, and save. The receipt can print, email, or download as PDF.

Pro tip: If you forget a shortcut, ask MartPoint Assist. It can check stock, find customers, and start a sale for you.

Quick fixes

Troubleshooting made simple

If something does not behave, try the safe step first. Most issues are fixed with a refresh, a cache clear, or a quick check.

Cannot log in

  • Check Caps Lock is off.
  • Make sure you are on the correct store link.
  • Ask the owner or admin to reset your password.
  • If the page does not load, check internet or Wi-Fi.

Blank or white page

  • Refresh the browser (Ctrl+R or Cmd+R).
  • Clear browser cache and cookies.
  • Try a different browser (Chrome is recommended).
  • Contact your admin or support if it persists.

Barcode does not scan

  • Wipe the scanner lens and barcode.
  • Make sure the scanner is in “keyboard” mode.
  • Try typing the code manually into the search box.
  • Confirm the item has a barcode saved in the system.

Receipt not printing

  • Check printer power, paper, and connection.
  • Tap the Print icon and wait for the browser dialog.
  • For thermal printers, confirm it is set as the default.
  • You can also email or download the receipt as PDF.

Sale will not save

  • Check all required fields are filled.
  • Make sure there is enough stock for the items.
  • Discounts over a limit may need a manager PIN.
  • If offline, the sale is queued and will sync automatically.

App feels slow

  • Close unused browser tabs or apps.
  • Clear cache and refresh.
  • Check internet/Wi-Fi signal strength.
  • Restart the browser or device.

Security

Idle timeout & screen lock

MartPoint can warn you and lock the screen when no one is using it. This protects your store data if a cashier or user walks away.

Idle warning

After the set number of minutes with no mouse, keyboard, or touch activity, a dialog appears and counts down. Tap Stay logged in to keep working.

Snooze screen

If the store owner enables Snooze, the screen freezes with a campaign/flyer instead of logging out. The user unlocks with their password or approval PIN.

Where it is set

Owners and managers set it in Store Settings → Session Lock. They choose the idle time in minutes, the warning countdown, and whether to show a campaign image.

What users should do

Do not leave a half-finished sale on screen. Put it on Hold or finish and save it before stepping away. If the screen snoozes, unlock it with your own password.

When you contact support

To help us solve your issue faster, please include:

  • Your store name and branch
  • What you were trying to do
  • The exact steps that led to the issue
  • A screenshot or error message if possible

Support coverage

MartPoint provides the software, documentation, and technical support. We do not supply computers, barcode scanners, receipt printers, internet service, or electricity. Those are provided by you or your implementation partner. We will, however, guide you on compatible hardware and setup best practices.
