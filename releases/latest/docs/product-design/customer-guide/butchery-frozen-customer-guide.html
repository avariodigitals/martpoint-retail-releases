Getting started

Before you start

This guide is for butcher shops and frozen food retailers. Make sure your MartPoint store is switched to the right business profile so the butchery and frozen tools appear.

Set the business type

Go to Settings → Business Profile and choose Butcher / Meat Shop or Frozen Foods Retailer.

Enable the right feature flags

Open the Feature Flags tab and turn on Warehouse, Meat / Butchery Workflow and/or Frozen Food Cold Chain.

Save and refresh

Click Save Business Profile and refresh. You will see Butchery & Frozen in the Operations sidebar.

Mobile access

On mobile, a Butchery tile appears under More so you can work in the cold room or at the cutting table.

Receiving

Receiving a carcass

Record every animal or bulk lot that enters your cold room before it is cut. The lot number is the foundation of traceability.

1

Open receive

Desktop: Operations → Butchery & Frozen → Receive Carcass. Mobile: More → Butchery → +.

2

Fill the details

Enter supplier, carcass name, lot/batch, receiving weight, slaughter date and expected yield %.

3

Choose the location and template

Pick the freezer or cold room. Optionally select a cutting template such as quarter-cow or whole cow.

4

Save

The carcass now appears as Received in the list, ready for cutting and customer shares.

Processing

Cutting a carcass

When the carcass is ready, open the cutting worksheet. Record actual weights and prices, then complete to create sellable items automatically.

Open the worksheet

From the carcass list, tap Cut. If you selected a template, the expected cuts appear automatically.

Record actual yield

For each cut enter: cut name, expected weight, actual weight, packs, waste, cost, sales price.

Complete the cut

Tap Complete Cutting. MartPoint marks the carcass completed and creates sellable items.

What is created

Each cut becomes an item with category Butchery Cuts, unit KG, stock in kg, lot, purchase and sales price.

Tip: You can sell these cuts at POS, on the online store or assign them to customer shares.

Reservations

Customer shares

Let customers reserve 1/2, 1/4, 1/8 or a custom fraction of an animal before it is cut. Ideal for cow sharing and group buys.

1

Open the share screen

Desktop: Butchery & Frozen → Carcass Shares. Mobile: More → Butchery → Shares.

2

Choose the carcass and customer

Select the received carcass and the customer. Enter the share fraction, reserved weight and amount.

3

Track deposit and status

Record deposit or full payment. Update the status from Reserved to Paid, Cut and finally Delivered.

Group buying

Add multiple customers to one carcass. The reservation list shows each person's fraction and deposit.

Fulfill shares

Mark each share delivered after hand-over. The carcass status keeps everyone on the same page.

Cold chain

Freezers & temperature logging

Frozen food businesses need to record storage temperature, thaw time and shelf life for compliance, quality and safety.

Add freezers

Desktop: Butchery & Frozen → Freezers. Mobile: More → Butchery → Cold Chain. Enter min/max temperature and capacity.

Log temperatures

Open the Temperature Log, pick the freezer and enter the current reading. Out-of-range readings are flagged.

Thaw & shelf life

For packaged frozen items, set thaw time, use-within window and frozen shelf life in the product form.

Traceability

Storage and temperature data follows the item and is available for compliance reports and recalls.

Sales & daily routines

Selling cuts & frozen products

Once a cut or frozen product is in the system, it behaves like any other item. It is ready for POS, online sales and batch tracking.

At the POS

Scan or select the cut, enter the weight and checkout. Stock decreases by the weight sold.

Online store

Publish the item to sell frozen packs and pre-ordered cuts. Lot numbers keep online orders traceable.

FEFO / batch tracking

Use batch/lot and expiry fields. MartPoint supports FEFO so older stock is sold first.

Best practices

Log temperatures twice a day, complete cuts on the same day and mark shares delivered after hand-over.
