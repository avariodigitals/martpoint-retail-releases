The most important commercial rule

MartPoint does not pay for names, contact details, meetings, demonstrations, quotations, verbal commitments or registered leads. A referral or sales commission can arise only when an eligible referred customer pays MartPoint through an approved payment route, the payment clears, the required licence activation is completed and the conditions in the partner’s signed Commission Schedule are satisfied.

Lead registration protects attribution. It does not create a debt or guaranteed commission.

Referral Partner

You introduce a genuinely new business that may benefit from MartPoint. MartPoint handles qualification, demonstration, quotation, payment collection, licensing, deployment and customer onboarding unless your agreement states otherwise.

Documents & tools you receive

  • Referral Partner Agreement and Commission Schedule.
  • Activation Confirmation and Partner ID.
  • Referral guide explaining eligible introductions and prohibited claims.
  • Approved customer-safe product and marketing materials.
  • Lead registration and status view for your own submissions.
  • Commission statement for eligible paid customer conversions.

How earnings work

StageWhat it means for you
Lead submittedRecorded for review. No commission exists.
Lead acceptedAttribution is protected for the stated period. No commission exists.
Customer interested or quotedSales activity is progressing. No commission exists.
Customer invoice issuedAn amount has been requested but not collected. No commission exists.
Customer pays MartPointThe transaction moves to payment clearance and activation checks.
Payment cleared & activation completedCommission may become eligible under the signed Commission Schedule and holding period.
Commission approved & paidThe amount appears on your partner statement with the payment reference.

Your boundaries

Referral status does not grant customer-account access. You must obtain consent before sharing a prospect’s details, use approved claims and never collect MartPoint licence payments or promise discounts, features or outcomes.

Channel Sales & Support Partner

You help suitable businesses understand and choose MartPoint and provide agreed first-line usage guidance to customers formally assigned to you. MartPoint remains responsible for quotations, billing, licensing, deployment and platform-level support.

Documents & tools you receive

  • Channel Partner Agreement and Commission Schedule.
  • Activation Confirmation, Partner ID and authorised status.
  • Approved sales deck, demo guidance, current pricing reference and qualification tools.
  • Opportunity registration, approved quotation support and sales handover process.
  • Customer Assignment for every customer you are permitted to support.
  • First-line support guide, escalation route, renewal tasks and partner statements.

How earnings work

No payment is made for an opportunity, meeting, demo, quotation or invoice alone. Acquisition commission is considered only after the eligible customer payment clears and activation conditions are completed.

Renewal commission applies only where your signed schedule provides for it and you remain assigned while completing the required customer-support and renewal activities.

Your boundaries

  • Access only assigned customers and approved information.
  • Do not alter prices or approve discounts.
  • Do not create licences or change entitlements.
  • Do not collect licence payments outside an approved route.
  • Do not deploy the platform or promise resolution times that MartPoint has not approved.

Implementation Partner

You perform a customer-specific implementation assignment after MartPoint has provisioned the environment. Work may include approved configuration, data preparation, user training, testing and handover.

Documents & tools you receive

  • Implementation Partner Agreement and certification confirmation.
  • Fee Schedule for standard work where applicable.
  • Customer-specific Work Order stating scope, deliverables, dates, fee, access, evidence and acceptance requirements.
  • Data Processing Agreement where the assignment requires personal-data processing.
  • Implementation checklist, training resources, testing requirements and handover form.
  • Time-limited access to the assigned customer functions required for the approved work.

How earnings work

Implementation work normally earns the fixed or milestone fee stated in the Work Order, not a percentage of the software licence. A fee becomes payable only after the applicable customer funds have cleared and MartPoint has accepted the required milestone, unless the Work Order states a different approved condition.

Your boundaries

  • Work only under an active Work Order.
  • MartPoint provisions and deploys the system and makes the final go-live decision.
  • Do not expand scope, retain access after the assignment or charge the customer outside the approved arrangement.

Technology Partner

You provide an approved hardware product, software service, API, connector, infrastructure service or embedded solution that works with MartPoint for a defined use case.

Documents & tools you receive

  • Mutual NDA where required and Technology Partner Agreement.
  • Commercial Schedule defining approved product, development, subscription, transaction or integration economics.
  • Solution Profile and the technical, security, privacy and support requirements for the approved product or version.
  • Integration Work Order or Statement of Work for development or customer-specific work.
  • Testing plan, approved documentation, controlled test access and production approval where applicable.
  • Service-level, warranty, maintenance, change-notice and incident procedures relevant to the solution.

How earnings work

Technology Partner status does not create a software-licence discount or commission. Earnings are limited to the product margin, development fee, subscription, integration fee or other basis stated in the signed Commercial Schedule. A separately registered referral is treated under an approved Referral Schedule only after the customer pays and all eligibility conditions are met.

Your boundaries

  • Approval is product, version and use-case specific.
  • It does not grant unrestricted API, production, tenant, source-code, server or customer-data access.
  • Public listing or co-marketing requires separate approval.

Payment Provider Partner

You enable an approved payment capability under the legal, technical, settlement, security and commercial responsibilities stated in the provider agreement.

Documents & tools you receive

  • Provider Agreement and Commercial Schedule.
  • Data Processing Agreement and service-level terms where applicable.
  • Settlement and Reconciliation Schedule.
  • Approved fund-flow and responsibility record.
  • Sandbox or test plan, acceptance results and Production Approval.
  • Named integration, finance, reconciliation and incident contacts.

How earnings work

No transaction fee, rebate, referral payment or revenue share should be assumed. Only the signed Provider Agreement and Commercial Schedule create payment rights and define the revenue base, deductions, taxes, settlement timing, reversals and reporting. A separate software referral follows the paid-customer rule.

Your boundaries

  • Maintain applicable authorisation, security, settlement, reconciliation, refund and chargeback obligations.
  • Access is limited to the approved payment service and the data required for that service.

Recognition

Partner grades

MartPoint may recognise sustained performance using four grades. Grade is recorded separately for each partner type, so an organisation may hold different grades for different roles.

Silver

Activated & compliant

Activated and meeting the programme’s baseline requirements.

Current badge, approved verification listing, core materials and standard support route.

Gold

Reliable performer

Demonstrating reliable, repeatable performance and good operational quality.

Gold badge, performance feedback, priority enablement and eligibility for approved case studies or co-marketing.

Platinum

Sustained excellence

Sustaining strong results, customer outcomes and programme compliance.

Platinum badge, business reviews, priority partner support and featured-listing consideration.

Diamond

Strategic value

Delivering outstanding strategic value with consistently high standards.

Diamond badge, executive engagement, strategic planning and consideration for suitable pilots.

How grade decisions work

MartPoint reviews relevant results, quality, customer experience, service performance, security and compliance. The partner receives its grade decision and next review date. Grade does not automatically change commission, fees, access, territory, exclusivity or lead allocation — any commercial change must be documented separately.

Maintaining your grade: serious misconduct, data misuse, security failure, misleading claims, regulatory failure, persistent service problems or inactivity may block promotion, lead to a lower grade or cause suspension. MartPoint will communicate the applicable decision and any required corrective action, subject to the partner agreement.

Working safely

Getting support

Protect customers & the MartPoint relationship

  • Use only your individual account and protect passwords and access devices.
  • Access only the customer, system and information specifically assigned to you.
  • Obtain consent before submitting prospect or customer information.
  • Use current approved descriptions, prices and marketing materials.
  • Report suspected security, privacy, fraud or access incidents immediately.
  • Do not collect MartPoint licence payments unless a written agreement authorises the exact route.
  • Do not present yourself as a MartPoint employee, agent with unlimited authority or exclusive representative.

Document priority: this guide explains the standard programme. Your signed Partner Agreement, Commercial or Commission Schedule, Work Order, Customer Assignment and technical schedules control your specific rights and obligations. If documents appear inconsistent, pause the affected activity and contact MartPoint for written clarification.

Before you begin

Confirm that your organisation name, Partner ID, approved type, grade, contacts, payout details and signed documents are correct. Do not begin customer-facing or technical activity until MartPoint confirms activation and any required assignment.
