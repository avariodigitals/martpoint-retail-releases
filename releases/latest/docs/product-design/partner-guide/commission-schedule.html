Commercial / Commission Schedule

The exact earning basis, eligible revenue, exclusions, clearance conditions, holding period, adjustments and payment process that apply to the named partner.

How to use: this schedule is completed per partner and signed alongside the Partner Agreement. It controls the partner’s specific commercial terms and prevails over the general programme guide.

3

Eligible revenue

Commission or fees are calculated only on cleared MartPoint licence payments made by an eligible referred or assigned customer through an approved payment route, after the required licence activation is completed.

4

Exclusions

  • Names, contact details, meetings, demonstrations, quotations, verbal commitments or registered leads.
  • Invoices issued but not paid, or payments that fail, are reversed or refunded.
  • Taxes (including VAT), hardware pass-through, third-party fees and expenses.
  • Payments collected outside an approved route, including any amount collected by the partner without written authorisation.

5

Clearance conditions & holding period

A commission or fee becomes eligible only when all of the following are satisfied:

  • Customer payment clears through the approved payment route.
  • Required licence activation is completed.
  • Holding period of days passes without refund or reversal.
  • Any further conditions in the signed schedule:
6

Adjustments

Refunds, chargebacks, failed activations and credit notes reduce or reverse the related commission or fee. MartPoint may offset adjustments against future payouts and will show each adjustment on the partner statement with a reference.
